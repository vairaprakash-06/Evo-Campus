Smart Campus Resource Optimizer

“Evolving Intelligence, Optimizing Tomorrow.” • Developed by Team EvoCampus

1. Project Overview & Objectives

Modern academic institutions face severe challenges in allocating finite physical campus assets—such as general lecture halls, specialized computer labs, hardware engineering rooms, and seminar auditoriums—among competing departmental requests. This scheduling challenge maps directly to the NP-hard Multi-Dimensional Knapsack and Timetabling Problem.

Objective 1: Multi-Constraint Satisfaction

Guarantee zero double-booking collisions, respect seating capacity limits, and enforce facility category matching.

Objective 2: Priority-Weighted Fairness

Protect critical capstone defenses and accredited practical labs using exponential weighting ($w \in [1, 11]$).

Objective 3: Occupancy & Budget Efficiency

Maximize seating occupancy ratio while minimizing unused seats and respecting departmental operational budgets.

2. Mathematical Fitness Function

$$\max_{\mathbf{X}} \mathcal{F}(\mathbf{X}) = 1000 + \sum_{i=1}^{N} \left[ w(P_i) \cdot 40 + \left( \frac{\text{Students}_i}{\text{Cap}(R_i)} \right) \cdot 25 \right] - \mathcal{P}_{\text{violations}}(\mathbf{X})$$

Where:
• $w(P_i)$ is the priority factor: Tier 1 (1.0), Tier 2 (2.0), Tier 3 (4.0), Tier 4 (7.0), Tier 5 (11.0).
• $\mathcal{P}_{\text{violations}}$ enforces heavy penalties: -500 for category mismatch, -450 per time-slot double booking collision, and -350 for capacity deficits.

3. Genetic Algorithm Operators

🧬 Chromosome Encoding

Vector of tuples $\mathbf{X} = [(R_1, T_1), (R_2, T_2), \dots, (R_N, T_N)]$ where each gene encodes the assigned facility ID and scheduled time slot.

🏆 Tournament Selection

Samples $k=3$ random chromosomes and selects the highest-fitness individual for parenthood, balancing exploitation and diversity.

✂️ Single-Point Crossover

Recombines parent chromosomes with probability $P_c \approx 0.85$, splicing allocation vectors across cut points.

🎲 Stochastic Gene Mutation

Perturbs genes with probability $P_m \approx 0.15$, reassigning facilities or swapping time slots to escape local optima.

👑 Elitism Preservation

Copies the top $e=2$ fittest candidate chromosomes unchanged into subsequent generations to prevent evolutionary regression.

🛠️ Feasibility Repair Engine

Intelligent repair operator resolves scheduling collisions and capacity breaches by dynamically re-routing sessions to compatible vacant slots.
